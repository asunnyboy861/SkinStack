SkinStack Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SkinStack is designed with privacy first. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our services:

  • Product Data: Skincare product names, brands, ingredients, and routine preferences you enter. This data is stored only on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Skin Journal Data: Skin condition ratings, concerns, and notes you log. This data is stored only on your device.
  • Photos: If you use the ingredient scanner feature, photos are processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos are never uploaded to any server.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising

3. In-App Purchases

Pro feature purchases are processed through Apple's App Store using StoreKit 2. We only receive a transaction confirmation from Apple. We do not have access to your full payment information.

4. Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no cloud sync, no server, and no external database. Your data remains under your full control.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual products or journal entries within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all stored data

6. Children's Privacy

SkinStack does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences interested in skincare.

7. Third-Party Services

SkinStack uses Apple's built-in frameworks only:

  • Vision: For on-device OCR text recognition (ingredient scanning)
  • SwiftData: For local data storage
  • StoreKit 2: For in-app purchases
  • UserNotifications: For timer completion alerts

None of these frameworks transmit your personal data externally.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
