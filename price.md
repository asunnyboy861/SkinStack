# Price Configuration

## Monetization Model
Free Download + One-Time Non-Consumable IAP (No Subscription)

## Pricing Details

### Free Tier (Always Available)
- Max 3 products
- Basic conflict detection (avoid-level only)
- Manual AM/PM assignment (no smart auto-assign)
- No OCR scanning
- No wait-time timer
- No skin journal
- No effect tracking charts

### Pro Tier - $4.99 One-Time Purchase (Non-Consumable)
- Unlimited products
- Full conflict detection (avoid + caution + neutral + synergistic)
- Smart AM/PM auto-allocation
- Vision OCR ingredient scanning
- Wait-time timer + push reminders
- Skin journal + photo comparison
- Effect tracking + trend charts
- Seasonal routine suggestions
- Product effectiveness rating tracking

3. Select "Non-Consumable"
4. Reference Name: SkinStack Pro
5. Product ID: com.zzoutuo.SkinStack.pro
6. Price: $4.99 (Tier 5)
7. Localization (English US):
   - Display Name: SkinStack Pro (max 30 chars)
   - Description: Unlock unlimited products, smart routines, and all premium features (max 45 chars)

## IAP Compliance Notes
- Non-consumable IAP: No auto-renewal, no subscription terms needed
- Restore Purchases button required
- No subscription management needed
- No free trial terms needed
- Privacy Policy link on paywall
- Terms of Use link on paywall

## Why Not Subscription?
1. Zero ongoing costs (no API, no cloud, no server)
2. Users hate subscriptions (FeelinMySkin reviews: biggest complaint)
3. One-time $4.99 < cost of 1 wasted serum bottle
4. Higher conversion rate vs subscription
5. ASO advantage: "No Subscription" is a selling point
