Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Overview

SkinStack ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Short version: We don't collect any personal data. Your skincare data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

SkinStack is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Skincare product data or routine information
  • Skin journal entries or photos
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs

All data you enter into SkinStack is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Camera and Photo Library Access

SkinStack may request access to:

  • Camera: Used exclusively for scanning product ingredient labels using on-device OCR (Vision framework). Images are processed locally and never transmitted.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you choose to attach photos to your skin journal entries. Photos are stored locally on your device.

In-App Purchases

SkinStack offers a one-time Pro upgrade ($4.99). Purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

SkinStack does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs or libraries that collect data on our behalf.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it benefits from Apple's built-in security features including device encryption and app sandboxing.

Children's Privacy

SkinStack is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any data at all, this policy applies to all users regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated revision date.
